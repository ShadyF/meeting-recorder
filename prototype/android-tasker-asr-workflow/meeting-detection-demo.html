Can three meeting signals yield one useful ASR prompt?

This explores whether a selected Calendar event, a foreground meeting app, and a matching meeting notification can prompt once without repeated prompts.

Important: this only models when to show a prompt. Tasker can prompt or launch ASR, but shortcut and auto-start behavior is unproven per device. One user tap to Start is an acceptable fallback; silent recording is not assumed to be reliable.
